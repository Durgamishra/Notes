Learning Roadmap

Your semester-by-semester learning journey through the BCA program.

Step 1: Semester Journey

Begin your degree with fundamental subjects and basic mathematics for computer science.

Step 2: Programming Fundamentals

Master logic building with C and C++ programming. Understand arrays, pointers, and OOP concepts.

Step 3: Core CS Subjects

Dive deep into Operating Systems, Database Management Systems, and Web Technologies.

Step 4: Data Science & AI

Explore modern technologies including Python, Data Science, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence.

Step 5: Development Skills

Build real-world projects, implement full-stack systems, and prepare for industry roles.
